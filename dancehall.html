what is dancehall.texas

dancehall.texas is where we post the real dancehall records that were played in the peak years of their releases. not covers. not remakes. the originals, when they ruled.

every post is a record, a person, or a moment in the music. the records. the artists. the tracks. the roots. we speak about all of it, so the history doesn't get lost.

● series 02 // the artists 8 slides

flourgon

michael may, jamaican deejay

born michael may in kingston, active from the mid-1980s. tutored by charlie chaplin, he deejayed on kingston sound systems and started his own, sweet love. debut: "big batty gal" (1986), produced by winston riley for techniques.

from the white hall avenue crew with red dragon, daddy lizard and sanchez.

beenie man, 1996

the closing track on maestro (december 10, vp / greensleeves), riding the punanny nowadays riddim from shocking vibes. twenty-one years later, g-eazy and kehlani's "good life" sampled it.

real years

the timeline so far

  • late 1970sin panama, artists of west indian ancestry start mixing jamaican reggae rhythms with spanish lyrics and panamanian folk music known as combos nacionales.
  • 1986flourgon's debut, "big batty gal," produced by winston riley for techniques.
  • 1988duck dance competition lands on king jammys records.
  • 1990el general's "tu pum pum" is reported as the first reggae en español song on us radio.
  • 1996beenie man's "romie" closes out maestro (december 10, vp / greensleeves), riding the punanny nowadays riddim.
  • 2017"good life" by g-eazy and kehlani samples "romie" on the fate of the furious soundtrack.
